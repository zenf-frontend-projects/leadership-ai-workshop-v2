So — what do we do?

There are two ways to use AI.
One compounds.

Mode 01

Builder

Uses AI to do their own work better. Drafts faster, researches deeper, codes more confidently. The output is theirs.

Linear · One person · One stream of work

Mode 02

Harness

A system built around AI that does the work itself. Triggered, runs, produces output. Humans only where judgment is needed.

Exponential · Runs while you sleep · Scales the org

How the work compounds — over the same week

Builders use AI.

Harnesses are AI.

Anatomy

A harness.

Model
Prompts what to ask
Tools how to act
Memory what it knows
Triggers when to run
Guardrails where to stop

The model is the engine. The harness is everything around it — the machine that turns raw capability into reliable, repeatable work.

The model is the engine.
The harness is the machine.

A harness is an engineering pattern.

It's also a leadership posture.

A harness, at the level of an organization.

01

Embedded operators.

AI builders sitting inside teams — understanding the process, paid to automate it. Not a central AI team consulting from the side.

02

AI KPIs on heads of department.

Adoption is owned where the work happens — not delegated to a function whose job is encouragement.

03

Patterns that compound across functions.

What works for one tribe becomes infrastructure for all of them. Tools, prompts, evals — shared.

Most companies are quietly losing ground here.

It's the kind of gap that compounds.
