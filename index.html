What is an agent harness?

Model
Prompts what to ask
Tools how to act
Memory what it knows
Triggers when to run
Guardrails where to stop

The model is the engine. The harness is everything around it — the machine that turns raw capability into reliable, repeatable work.

06

A harness is an engineering practice.

It's also an organisational mindset.

07
Mode 01

Builder

Uses AI to do their own work better. Drafts faster, researches deeper, codes more confidently. Works in-the-loop, compounds linearly.

08
Mode 02

Harness

Builds systems around AIs that do work for you. Creates efficiency, scale and leverage. Works on-the-loop, compounds exponentially.

09

Builder vs Harness.

10

Builders create output.

Harnesses create leverage.

11

Harnesses as an org mindset.

01

Embedded operators.

AI-native builders sitting inside teams who understand processes, and are incentivised to build AI solutions around them.

02

Compounding behaviours.

Teams work more on-the-loop, less in-the-loop. Processes become agents, standards become skills, AI-readable documents become the non-negotiable source of truth for agents. Create leverage, not output.

03

AI Roadmaps as a KPI.

A concrete AI roadmap per function, reviewed the way other strategic plans already are.
