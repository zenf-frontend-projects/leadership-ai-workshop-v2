What do we do about it?

There are two ways to use AI.
Only one of them compounds.

Builder

Uses AI to do their own work better. Drafts faster, researches deeper, codes more confidently. The output is theirs.

Compounds linearly. One person, one stream of work.

Harness

A system built around AI that does the work itself. Triggered by something. Runs through steps. Produces an output. A human is in the loop only where judgment is needed.

Compounds exponentially. Runs while you sleep. Scales across the org.

Builder

One person, linear.

Harness

One system, exponential.

Builders use AI.

Harnesses are AI.

Before we go further, a word about the word.

The model is the engine. The harness is everything around it that turns raw capability into reliable, repeatable work.

The model is the engine.
The harness is the machine.

A harness is an engineering pattern.

It is also a leadership posture.

Harness, at the level of an organization.

Embedded AI operators.

People sitting inside teams, deeply understanding the process, incentivized to automate it. Not a central AI team that consults to everyone.

AI KPIs on heads of department.

The metric of AI adoption is owned where the work happens. Not delegated to a function whose job is to encourage AI.

Org-level skills and frameworks.

Patterns and tools that compound across functions. What works for one tribe becomes infrastructure for all of them.

Most companies are quietly losing ground here.

It is the kind of gap that compounds the longer it goes unaddressed.
